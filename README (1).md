# Seyi Makinde 2027 Campaign Strategy & Electoral Simulator

An interactive 36-state + FCT strategic electoral simulation dashboard and Python Monte Carlo engine evaluating Nigeria's 2027 electoral cycle through the lens of **Seyi Makinde's** strategic outlook. Built for non-technical decision-makers, campaign advisors, and quantitative portfolio reviewers.

## Live Simulator
* **Interactive Dashboard:** `https://favour-emmanuel-omolafe.github.io/makinde-2027-campaign-simulator/`

## Core Capabilities & Electoral Framework
* **5-Bloc Multi-Party Battlefield:** Models vote shares across **APM (Makinde)**, **APC (Incumbent)**, **NDC (Obi/Kwankwaso)**, **ADC (Atiku)**, and **PDP** across all 36 states plus the FCT.
* **Section 134 Constitutional Spread:** Tracks both national vote share and the constitutional threshold of **at least 25% in 24 or more of the 37 jurisdictions**.
* **Interactive Strategic Levers:**
  * **Candidate Geopolitical Zone & One-Term Transitional Pledge:** Simulates South-West intra-zone dynamics, power-rotation friction, and coalition alignment.
  * **774-LGA Preparation Runway:** Models grassroots ward and polling-unit readiness across short sprints and multi-year builds.
  * **Polling-Unit Agent Retention & Rigging Friction:** Stress-tests ballot defense and asymmetric manipulation risks in unguarded states, with home-state insulation in **Oyo** and **Bauchi**.
  * **6.1M Organic Surge Stress Test:** Evaluates what happens if APM replicates a large organic wave without unified opposition pooling.
  * **Oyo Feb 6 Safety Index:** Estimates down-ballot spillover risk from the presidential contest into the February 6, 2027 Oyo State governorship handover.
